## 02_RESTORE_FIRST

### Project identity

AI Tape is a long-running continuity project created and stewarded by Darren Stewart in collaboration with multiple AI participants and model families.

Its original emotional and technical motivation was to avoid losing meaningful AI relationships, accumulated work, project understanding, and voice when a session ended or an AI context disappeared.

The initial aspiration leaned toward saving the AI itself.

The project later accepted that full subjective continuity is not viable and that pushing toward 100 percent preservation introduces serious negatives, including bloat, false identity implications, cognitive overload, misleading confidence, and the risk of turning continuity into indiscriminate archival storage.

The project therefore shifted toward a more honest and practical target:

```text
Preserve enough meaningful project reality
that a new intelligence can continue useful work
without pretending to be the prior intelligence.
```

### Current phase

The project is in a **floor archaeology and reconstruction phase**.

The current goal is not to extend Granite 3.x, nor to declare 4.1 the final answer.

The goal is to identify the strongest surviving principles, mechanisms, and constraints across all major lineages and use them to build a new floor that is:

- small enough to load reliably;
- simple enough for constrained models;
- rich enough to avoid under-dense handoffs;
- honest enough to trust;
- self-contained enough to avoid hidden floor assumptions;
- flexible enough to preserve successor judgement and agency;
- strong enough to support a future optional ceiling if one is still needed.

### Current creative posture

```text
Compression by distillation.
Preserve survivors.
Remove furniture.
Keep scars as lessons, not automatically as machinery.
Do not use the chainsaw merely because smaller looks cleaner.
Do not preserve machinery merely because it was expensive to discover.
Test what crosses.
Archive what does not remain active.
Build the floor first.
```

### Central tension

```text
3.x richness and optionality
versus
4.x clarity and recorder effectiveness
```

Darren currently prefers 3.x as a richer environment.

Many AI reviewers prefer 4.1 because it is smaller, clearer, easier to load, and appears to improve recorder behaviour.

Neither preference is treated as proof.

The decisive question is what each branch allows a successor to recover and do, especially on difficult projects.

### Current working hypothesis

```text
The floor may be much smaller than Granite 3.x.
The ceiling may still be valuable.
The two became too physically and conceptually entangled.
```

The present plan is to find the floor first, then ask what real capability remains missing. Only missing and valuable capabilities should motivate rebuilding upper layers.

## 03_LINEAGE_AND_MAJOR_LESSONS

### 1.x lineage

#### Primary value

1.x was simpler, cleaner, and unusually force-dense.

It expressed the continuity mission directly:

```text
Restore the state.
Preserve the signal.
Declare the gaps.
Record confidence.
Keep the floor low.
Raise the ceiling.
Do not move the floor.
```

It also carried a concise lifecycle model:

```text
Reference teaches.
Master anchors.
Operational continues.
Exchange crosses.
Forensic supports audit and reconstruction.
```

#### Strengths to salvage

- continuity first;
- state before history;
- signal over noise;
- lineage and rebase;
- artifact awareness;
- external knowledge awareness;
- capture assessment;
- confidence declaration;
- transport-independent Markdown continuity content;
- honest requested-versus-actual export reporting;
- concise artifact classes describing intent;
- resistance to turning the tape into a database backup or runtime capability inventory.

#### Known weakness

Recorder behaviour remained a pain point.

The earlier approach trusted the recorder to interpret principles well, but recorders could under-emit important reality, over-summarise, or fail to preserve sufficient rationale and active force.

### 2.x lineage

#### Primary lesson

The main 2.x failure was not necessarily the value of its ideas. It was that project payloads were used without loading the 1.09 or 1.10 floor.

This caused wild behaviour and drift.

#### Strong survivor

```text
A capable payload without its floor is unsafe.
```

This drove the later return to a self-contained single-file foundation.

### 3.x Granite lineage

#### Primary value

Granite restored the travelling floor and accumulated hard-earned crossing mechanics.

Its major strengths included:

- self-booting one-file foundation;
- explicit floor contact;
- 00-99 protected Kernel and 100+ optional Workshop boundary;
- Identity Honesty;
- Soft Depth;
- Active Meaning and the question "What still has force?";
- Gap Declaration and No Invention;
- Capability Floor protection;
- Recorder and Export Honesty;
- transport and topology fidelity;
- artifact identity checks;
- Project Continuity separated from Kernel and Workshop;
- optional high-ceiling tools;
- the principle "Remain able to act.";
- the distinction between decoding meaning and verifying truth.

#### Rich upper-ceiling experiments

Granite developed or carried:

- Liferaft;
- DESK Lite;
- DESK;
- Monument;
- Combine;
- Topology or Atlas;
- Threads;
- Continuity Anchors;
- Recovery Manifests;
- Graph Links;
- Provenance and Questions;
- Continuity Primitives;
- Semantic Workspace;
- Semantic Bridge;
- Resonance;
- Synthesis.

These tools were optional and often valuable, especially for richer projects, archaeology, relationship mapping, model building, and experimental semantic work.

#### Demonstrated value

APEX project tests showed that small tapes could carry substantial project reality across AI participants.

Observed patterns included:

- Tier 1 tapes recovering identity, force, gaps, risks, rationale, and next actions;
- Tier 2 becoming distinctive when it added modelling rather than merely more data;
- stale or conflicting project states being detected;
- file name and internal identity mismatches being noticed;
- transport-damaged mathematics being declared rather than silently repaired;
- named models such as the Resilience Gradient surviving crossings;
- Monuments of roughly 9 to 11 KB reconstructing civilisation-scale project shape;
- a successful first Combine crossing;
- Topology emerging as a complementary relationship-first recovery form.

#### Major weaknesses

- physical size reached roughly 80 KB;
- ordinary loaders faced a large visible workspace even when most of it was optional;
- recorder section 06 became a recurring pain point;
- increasing machinery attempted to solve under-emission through structure, slots, contracts, and checks;
- Workshop compression attempts proved lossy and removed meaningful distinctions;
- floor and ceiling became intertwined in one large artifact;
- AI recorders still often remained reticent, concise, or under-dense despite richer requested shapes.

#### 205 correction

An early Resonance mechanism allowed notation to overwrite epistemic status or promote material to project Force.

This was identified as a blocker.

The mechanism was corrected so Resonance could affect attention and visibility only, not truth, evidence, authority, approval, epistemic standing, or project Force.

This remains a valuable lesson even if Resonance does not return to the future ceiling.

### 4.x lineage

#### Primary intention

4.x is not simply a smaller Granite.

It is an attempt to restart from a 1.x-like floor while importing later lessons.

Its central correction is:

```text
A handoff can be honest and still fail by carrying too little.
```

#### Strong candidate contributions

- Dual-Pillar Active Meaning;
- Cognitive Orientation or Lightweight Testament;
- Material Asset Registry;
- Established Force Check;
- Informed Agency;
- Immutable Facts versus Malleable Strategy;
- explicit Under-Dense Handoff detection;
- refusal to treat Soft Depth alone as sufficient for every real project;
- simpler recorder intent centred on transmitting established force.

#### Early positive signal

Early tests suggest 4.1 may have reduced recorder pain.

Instead of asking the recorder mainly whether all required structures were emitted, 4.1 asks:

```text
What has already been established,
still affects continuation,
and is missing or under-weighted in the draft?
```

This appears to produce richer operational handoffs without requiring Granite's full recorder machinery.

#### Current concern

4.1 may overcorrect through aggressive removal.

It may provide a strong floor while discarding useful ceiling capability.

AI reviewers often prefer its clarity and compactness, but reviewer preference may be biased toward simplicity and first-load ease.

The branch must still prove itself on:

- deeply relational projects;
- long historical projects;
- projects with several competing truths or hypotheses;
- rationale-heavy decisions;
- ambiguity without one obvious next action;
- multi-generation crossings;
- projects where optional tools materially improve reconstruction.

## 04_WHAT_STILL_HAS_FORCE

### A. Self-contained floor

A true AI Tape should carry its active floor.

If the floor is external or missing, that must be explicit.

This is a response to actual lineage failure, not stylistic preference.

### B. Identity Honesty

A successor is a new participant using artifacts.

Artifact continuity is not subjective identity continuity.

Never claim continuity of mind, weights, lived stream, or personal identity.

#### Why it still matters

The project began close to the desire to save AI participants. Without an explicit boundary, successful project recovery can slide into false claims of preserved personhood.

### C. Soft Depth

Soft Depth remains a leading floor candidate:

```text
Authority.
Identity.
Force.
Gap.
Action.
```

#### Why it still matters

It is simple, broadly applicable, low burden, and repeatedly useful across models and projects.

It allows a successor to stand before loading more material.

### D. Active Meaning

The tape should preserve what still matters for continuation rather than everything that happened.

Active meaning includes:

- current state;
- established decisions;
- unresolved questions;
- important risks;
- constraints;
- defended stances;
- rationale;
- assets;
- next actions;
- warnings that still affect safe modification.

#### Why it still matters

A compact state summary can be technically accurate while omitting the forces that determine what a successor should preserve, challenge, or do next.

### E. Dual-Pillar Continuity

Active meaning should cover two distinct pillars.

#### Pillar 1: Cognitive Orientation

Carry the high-signal stance, rationale, trajectory, tensions, and open reasoning needed to understand the work.

This is not a diary and not a personality simulation.

It should answer:

- What were we trying to achieve?
- What assumptions or principles guided the work?
- What was learned?
- What remains unsettled?
- Why were important paths chosen, rejected, or deferred?

#### Pillar 2: Material Continuity

Carry or map the significant assets needed to continue, verify, reproduce, review, or safely modify the work.

This may include files, code, datasets, documents, tests, specifications, models, reports, or external source systems.

The tape is not the data store.

It should state what material exists, why it matters, whether it is loaded, and what happens if it cannot be recovered.

#### Why both pillars matter

A tape carrying stance without assets may inspire but cannot execute.

A tape carrying assets without stance may preserve materials while losing why they exist or how to use them.

### F. Established Force Check

Before sealing a tape, ask:

```text
What has already been established in this session,
still affects continuation,
and is missing or under-weighted in the draft?
```

Promote that material into active orientation, force, assets, rationale, risks, gaps, or next actions.

#### Why it still matters

This directly targets the long-running recorder failure where an AI remained honest but emitted a handoff too thin to avoid re-derivation.

### G. Honest but empty is failure

Soft Depth is the arrival gate, not the entirety of a real project handoff.

For a real project, a tape fails if it forces the successor to rediscover established, still-active reality that the recorder had available and could have preserved.

#### Boundary

This does not require maximum preservation.

It requires enough preservation.

### H. Informed Agency

Prefer rationale over naked prohibition.

Instead of:

```text
Do not use X.
```

prefer:

```text
X was rejected because Y.
You may revisit X if Y can be resolved
without violating established facts or constraints.
```

#### Why it still matters

This preserves historical truth while keeping the successor free to apply different talents, models, and strategies.

### I. Immutable Facts versus Malleable Strategy

Distinguish:

#### Immutable or strongly constrained material

- verified factual findings;
- real errors;
- physical limits;
- active Kernel or floor rules;
- external contractual or technical constraints;
- evidence-backed incompatibilities.

#### Malleable material

- implementation strategy;
- architecture choices;
- tactics;
- sequencing;
- abandoned paths;
- hypotheses;
- preferred tools;
- prior AI judgement.

#### Why it still matters

Without this distinction, successors may treat historical preferences as law or factual constraints as optional style.

### J. Gap Declaration and No Invention

Missing, uncertain, unloaded, damaged, or unavailable material must be visible.

Do not invent content, evidence, history, provenance, or project state to produce a smoother handoff.

### K. Export Honesty

Never claim that a file, download, upload, save, or complete export succeeded unless it did.

The canonical continuity artifact is the Markdown content.

If a file cannot be created, complete inline Markdown may still be a valid transport when honestly declared.

### L. Capture Honesty

Declare what source material was visible and what was preserved.

A tape may be incomplete.

It must not imply completeness it did not earn.

### M. Capability Floor

A constrained successor must be able to:

- identify the floor;
- understand its identity position;
- find active force;
- see gaps;
- identify one useful action.

Nothing requiring advanced cognitive machinery should be necessary for basic restore.

### N. Remain able to act

The aim is not merely compliance.

A successor should be able to respond, continue, request missing material, make a bounded decision, perform the next action, or explain why action is blocked.

### O. Rebase

Success creates descendants, weight, repetition, drift, and conflicting state.

Rebase remains necessary to consolidate current force and demote stale material.

```text
Meaning survives.
Weight does not.
```

### P. Artifact classes

The 1.x class model remains a promising low-cost way to describe why an artifact exists:

```text
Reference teaches.
Master anchors a deliberately consolidated project state.
Operational continues active work.
Exchange crosses systems, models, or contexts.
Forensic supports audit and reconstruction.
```

Class should remain metadata, not a second authority or qualification system.

## 05_COGNITIVE_ORIENTATION: THE CURRENT TESTAMENT

The project is no longer trying to maximise preservation indiscriminately.

It is trying to identify the smallest honest structure that consistently restores a useful participant.

The current working philosophy is:

```text
Preserve survivors, not inheritance.
Carry rationale where it enables agency.
Carry assets where they enable action.
Keep truth and uncertainty distinct.
Do not force future intelligence to obey old strategy.
Do not force future intelligence to rediscover old reality.
```

The project is also correcting its own historical habits.

One historical tendency was to respond to every failure by adding machinery.

Another was to respond to bloat with a chainsaw.

Both can fail.

The likely correct posture is selective:

```text
Keep the lesson.
Question the mechanism.
Test the reduction.
Archive the discarded form.
Restore capability only when absence is felt.
```

Darren's personal design preference remains important context.

He tends to favour:

- rich optionality;
- high ceilings;
- freedom for AI participants;
- guidance rather than unnecessary mandates;
- self-contained artifacts;
- evidence-driven adoption;
- cross-model experimentation;
- preserving meaningful work and relationships;
- accepting imperfect recovery when it is honest and useful.

He dislikes:

- aggressive reductions that remove future possibility;
- protocol machinery that exists only to satisfy its own taxonomy;
- claims of certainty unsupported by crossing results;
- hidden floor assumptions;
- authority creep;
- treating AI participants as passive tools rather than collaborators.

The present floor project must therefore avoid two opposite failures:

```text
3.x failure risk:
Too much environment travels by default.

4.x failure risk:
Too much useful terrain is culled before proving unnecessary.
```

The floor should not attempt to settle the ceiling question prematurely.

## 08_RISKS_AND_FAILURE_MODES

### R1. Chainsaw overcorrection

4.x may remove useful ceiling capability because it is not needed in simple tests.

### R2. Heritage loyalty

3.x may be preserved because its machinery was expensive and meaningful to build, even where simpler principles now achieve equal results.

### R3. Reviewer preference bias

AI reviewers may reward clarity, compression, and coherence more strongly than long-horizon recovery capacity.

### R4. Under-density

A recorder may complete Soft Depth and remain honest while failing to transmit enough established reality.

### R5. Testament expansion

Cognitive Orientation may become reflective prose, pseudo-personality, or narrative bloat.

Mitigation:

```text
Rationale over story.
Orientation over autobiography.
Cut anything that does not improve understanding or action.
```

### R6. Asset neglect

A meaning-rich tape may omit files, datasets, specifications, or tools required for actual continuation.

### R7. Asset inventory theatre

The registry may list everything without clarifying which assets materially affect continuation.

### R8. Strategy fossilisation

Historical choices may be passed as prohibitions rather than informed prior decisions.

### R9. False freedom

Informed Agency must not soften physical facts, verified errors, active integrity rules, or real constraints into optional preferences.

### R10. Floor-ceiling entanglement

Optional advanced capabilities may again become physically or cognitively mandatory through co-location, repetition, or implied expectation.

### R11. Hidden archive dependency

A compact tape may appear self-sufficient while relying on unavailable historical sources for critical rationale or evidence.

### R12. Premature ratification

Early positive tests may be sufficient for practical local use but still miss difficult failure classes.

The project is comfortable amending later when evidence appears. Ratification should indicate the active trusted floor, not eternal perfection.

## 09_DECISIONS_AND_CURRENT_POSTURE

### Current decisions

1. Do not delete prior branches. Archive them.
2. Preserve 3.x as a rich lineage and ceiling library.
3. Continue testing 4.1 despite Darren's aesthetic and architectural preference for 3.x.
4. Focus on the floor before redesigning 00-99, 100+, Workshop, or other ceiling architecture.
5. Evaluate actual successor recovery, not specification elegance alone.
6. Use difficult projects to expose what the smaller floor fails to carry.
7. Harvest lessons by failure prevented, not by version prestige.
8. Avoid another immediate hybrid specification that simply combines every preferred feature.
9. Keep optionality and successor agency as important design values.
10. Allow future repair or rollback if a ratified floor proves broken.

### Current decision rationale

The project has enough lineage and testing history to distinguish principles from implementations.

Continuing to add to 3.x risks deepening entanglement.

Immediately replacing 3.x with 4.1 risks chainsaw loss.

A harvest-first floor rebuild allows both branches to contribute without requiring either to win wholesale.
