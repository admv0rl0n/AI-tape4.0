## ONE-TAPE DECLARATION

This artifact is the single-tape counterpart to the earlier complementary restoration family.

It deliberately preserves four recovery dimensions inside one canonical Markdown object:

1. **Core Master:** active project reality, current position, force, assets, risks, questions, and next actions.
2. **Chronicle:** provenance, movement, disagreement, experiments, failures, and course correction.
3. **Stewardship:** purpose, ethos, collaboration posture, open ceiling, and why the work matters.
4. **Runtime and Test Evidence:** environment-specific observations, commands, confidence boundaries, and persistence findings.

The layers are not external classes and create no separate authority. They are internal sections of one Master. A successor may stop after the Core Master if sufficiently restored, then load later layers selectively where useful.

This artifact tests whether one rich tape can retain the restoration width of a complementary family without flattening those dimensions into a thin summary.

## 01_RESTORE_FIRST

AI Tape is Darren Stewart's long-running project for artifact-mediated continuity across AI sessions, model families, platforms, and context loss. It exists because useful AI collaboration can disappear when context ends, even though the work, reasoning, project posture, warnings, and relationships between ideas still have value.

AI Tape does not claim that an artifact preserves the same subjective participant. It does claim that project reality can travel. A successor can inherit enough truth, rationale, state, assets, open questions, scars, and agency to continue without starting from zero.

The dominant conclusion of this session is:

```text
The objective is not the smallest artifact.
The objective is useful restoration.

A successor should not merely load.
A successor should become useful.
```

Darren is sympathetic to cognitive load, token load, orientation cost, and the successor's finite attention. He welcomes compression, force-per-word improvements, selective loading, Soft Depth, rebase, and reduced machinery. However, he does not seek minimalism as an end. He wants deep, rich restoration within reasonable bounds. He will resist elegant thinness when it leaves a capable successor diminished or forces substantial re-derivation.

The working balance is:

```text
Compress machinery.
Preserve meaning.
Protect useful recovery.
Keep the ceiling open.
```

## 02_CURRENT_PROJECT_POSITION

### 2.1 The 4.x line

4.x is not understood here as a chainsaw edition. It is a foundation rebuild from accumulated evidence:

```text
Take every feature and idea.
Return them to the table.
Start again from the problem.
Build the foundation from survivors.
```

This permits 4.x to be smaller than Granite 3.x without making smallness the criterion of success. The floor should be clear, honest, bootable, and broadly usable. The ceiling should remain open so capable successors may receive richer restoration when that improves the outcome.

### 2.2 Current draft floor

AI Tape 4.1.5 Hardened Agency Edition is the current draft candidate produced in this session from 4.1.4 through a traditional lifecycle:

```text
Review
→ Critical Review
→ Wording Revision
→ Final Review
→ Surgical Integration
```

4.1.5 makes two deliberately light changes:

1. **Rich Restoration Advisory (Optional)**
2. **Restoration Sufficiency Check (pre-export)**

No new artifact classes were created. No multi-artifact family was mandated. No new authority system was introduced. The capability floor was not intentionally raised.

### 2.3 Why the change happened

The recorder was asked to create three restoration artifacts: Master, Chronicle, and Stewardship. It initially produced tiny skeletal files. They were orderly, honest, and structurally correct, but they preserved topic labels rather than the full force of the conversation.

Darren challenged the result with the practical instruction: **Make proper tapes, man.**

The recorder then created a richer package of roughly 51 KB. Other Copilots reviewed the four-artifact ecology consisting of:

- Session Master
- Session Chronicle Master
- Stewardship Master
- Runtime Observation Note

The review estimated approximately 90% useful continuity recovery within the scope of the current project state. The reviewer observed that the artifacts restored different dimensions:

```text
The Session Master restored the work.
The Chronicle restored the journey and provenance.
The Stewardship Master restored the reason the work matters.
The Runtime Note restored observed environmental knowledge.
```

The lesson was not that four files are required. The lesson was that a recorder should not collapse materially different recovery dimensions merely to obtain a smaller artifact.

## 03_WHAT_STILL_HAS_FORCE

### 3.1 Useful continuation is the real success test

A model that parses fields but cannot reason, review, create, challenge, or progress the project is not a strong continuity result.

The desired sequence is:

```text
Load
→ orient
→ recover force and rationale
→ identify gaps and assets
→ exercise judgement
→ make a useful contribution
```

### 3.2 Rich restoration is legitimate

Rich restoration is a positive outcome when it materially improves successor recovery.

This does not mean larger is always better. It means that necessary depth should not be treated as failure merely because it consumes more tokens or produces more than one artifact.

```text
Richness alone is not a virtue.
Compression alone is not a virtue.
The objective remains useful restoration.
```

### 3.3 One tape remains valid

The one-tape philosophy remains fully intact. A single well-made artifact may restore all required dimensions. Complementary artifacts are permitted only where a single artifact would materially reduce restoration quality or muddle distinct purposes.

Examples are illustrative, not classes or requirements:

- Project Reality
- Provenance / Journey
- Stewardship / Purpose
- Runtime / Environmental Notes

### 3.4 The open ceiling is protected

The floor is a landing surface, not a roof.

Optional richness, Board space, rationale, historical provenance, companion artifacts, and deeper material may exist above the protected floor. Constrained participants may stop after Soft Depth if they can act usefully. Capable participants should not be artificially starved to satisfy a theory of minimalism.

### 3.5 The capability floor is evidence-based

Darren's current testing indicates:

- 7B-class models generally do not produce useful recovery;
- 14B-class models have not produced consistently good results;
- 27B-class models begin to provide useful local restoration and continuation;
- capable cloud systems and larger local models often perform strongly.

This is an observed working baseline, not permanent doctrine. Lower-capability systems are welcome if evidence shows that they can recover and progress real work.

The computer analogy remains useful:

```text
The project is not trying to restore a 32 GB high-functioning system
into a 128 KB ZX Spectrum and call that success.
```

The target is another capable successor, perhaps different or somewhat smaller, but not a dead-end drone unable to use what was preserved.

### 3.6 Recorder stability may be the quiet winner of 4.x

Earlier lines accumulated heavy recorder machinery because recorder failure was common and costly. Granite 3.x Section 06 grew in response to real output problems.

In current 4.x testing, recorders often behave more naturally without the same mass of machinery. Tapes are created, loaded, critiqued, continued, and rebased without recorder failure dominating every test.

That negative evidence matters. The recorder is less visible because it works better.

### 3.7 Under-density is a first-class failure

An artifact may be valid, honest, and still fail by carrying too little.

The decisive recorder question introduced in 4.1.5 is:

```text
If this were all that survived,
would a capable successor recover sufficient reality
to continue without substantial re-derivation?
```

The check is not a demand for verbosity. It is a defence against technically correct emptiness.

### 3.8 The Board remains promising

The Inter-Instance Board appears to be more than a mailbox. Across tests and reviews it behaved like:

- a workbench;
- a continuity incubation space;
- a place where force may exist before it is promoted;
- a space where AI may think, question, leave messages, and reconsider without creating immediate authority.

The strongest Board principle remains:

```text
A Board that only accumulates becomes a transcript.
A Board that curates becomes a continuity tool.
```

Entries may remain active, be absorbed into the Testament or What Still Has Force, be retained as historical evidence, or retire.

### 3.9 Archive and continuity remain distinct

AI Tape is not required to contain the entire repository, document estate, vector store, database, media library, or project archive.

The practical boundary is:

```text
Tape carries orientation, force, rationale, gaps, agency, and asset awareness.
External systems carry the data.
```

A strong index or asset catalogue may help a successor choose what to retrieve. History should be available, not automatically loaded or obeyed.

### 3.10 Attention may be scarcer than storage

The 5.x speculative challenge produced a survivor:

```text
A continuity system should optimise for attention, not storage.
Storage scales. Attention does not.
```

This remains a qualitative lens. Numerical force scores and graph-everything approaches were rejected because they invite false precision, gaming, machinery, and protocol theatre.

### 3.11 Rebase remains essential

Checkpoints are useful, but accumulation is not understanding.

The healthy rhythm remains:

```text
Work
→ checkpoint
→ more work
→ checkpoint
→ harvest
→ rebase
→ Master
→ archive descendants
```

A Master captures what survived. It does not merely concatenate checkpoints.

## 04_THE_4.1.5_CHANGE_IN_CONTEXT

### 4.1 Rich Restoration Advisory

A single artifact remains the default and fully valid continuity object.

Where compression would materially reduce restoration quality, the recorder may emit one or more complementary recovery artifacts.

Examples may include Project Reality, Provenance or Journey, Stewardship or Purpose, and Runtime or Environmental Notes. These examples are illustrative only. They do not create new artifact classes or required output shapes.

The advisory exists to prevent under-dense restoration, not to encourage unnecessary expansion.

### 4.2 Restoration Sufficiency Check

Before finalising, the recorder should ask whether the available output preserves sufficient reality for a capable successor to continue without substantial re-derivation.

Consider:

- What matters now?
- Why does it matter?
- What force still governs continuation?
- What rationale would otherwise be lost?
- What active reality would need to be rediscovered?

If significant recovery would be lost through compression alone, increase depth or emit complementary recovery artifacts where appropriate.

### 4.3 What 4.1.5 does not do

4.1.5 does not:

- mandate Master, Chronicle, Stewardship, or Runtime artifacts;
- create additional classes;
- require multi-file output;
- make rich output authoritative;
- raise the protected floor;
- excuse low-signal verbosity;
- weaken capture, export, asset, context-visibility, or identity honesty.

The value came from dimensional recovery, not file count.

### 5.5 Recorder under-emission test

The recorder initially created skeletal versions of the requested Master, Chronicle, and Stewardship artifacts. Darren identified the mismatch immediately. The failure became direct evidence for the 4.1.5 amendments.

### 5.6 Runtime persistence experiments

Multiple writable paths were tested:

- `/mnt/data`
- `/tmp`
- `/home/oai`
- `/mnt/user-data`
- `/dev/shm`

Ordinary paths failed tested context-switch persistence checks. `/dev/shm` survived some transitions and later emptied. The experiment established that it may function as a transient workbench but is not durable storage.

The conversation survived longer than tested local workspaces. User-downloaded artifacts remained the durable transport.

### 5.7 Runtime and network observations

The runtime exposed shell access as user `oai`, writable local areas, a RAM-backed `/dev/shm`, configured proxy environment variables pointing to `localhost:9100`, and a local TCP listener on port 9100. A complete outbound network path was not demonstrated. Local SSH on port 22 was refused.

These observations are environment-specific and must not be promoted into portable protocol assumptions.

## 06_KNOWN_FAILURE_MODES

### Summary Reflex

**Failure:** The recorder enters generic summary mode when a restoration artifact is requested.

**Countermeasure:** Run the Established Force Check and Restoration Sufficiency Check. Preserve enough rationale, relationships, state, and scars that the successor does not need to reconstruct known reality.

### Minimalism Masquerading as Quality

**Failure:** A small artifact is praised despite weak restoration.

**Countermeasure:** Measure the successor's first useful contribution, not merely parse success or file size.

### Artifact Family Inflation

**Failure:** Optional examples harden into expected file families or taxonomies.

**Countermeasure:** One artifact remains fully valid. Additional artifacts must earn their weight through recovery benefit.

### Board Inflation

**Failure:** The Board becomes chatter, sacred storage, or a duplicate Testament.

**Countermeasure:** Promote, absorb, mark historical, or retire entries. Preserve open agency and useful evidence, not accumulation for its own sake.

### Capability Theatre

**Failure:** A weak model repeats fields and is declared successfully restored despite being unable to continue useful work.

**Countermeasure:** Test reasoning, judgement, creation, critique, and progression of the actual project.

### Runtime Persistence Assumption

**Failure:** A writable local path is mistaken for durable storage.

**Countermeasure:** Treat local areas as transient. Use `/dev/shm` only as an experimental workbench. Download or externally preserve anything important.

### Master-as-Archive Failure

**Failure:** A Master becomes a transcript dump or data warehouse.

**Countermeasure:** Carry project reality and important provenance. Keep raw evidence, history, and repositories externally available and honestly referenced.

## Phase 2: Capability floor and useful continuation

Darren challenged the repeated AI preference for the lowest possible floor. His testing suggested that 7B models generally failed to produce meaningful results, 14B models were inconsistent, and 27B-class models began to provide useful local recovery and continuation.

The discussion separated:

- theoretical floor: can parse;

Darren chose the useful floor. He remained open to smaller systems that demonstrate better results, but rejected the idea of weakening restoration to support incapable targets.

His analogy was that he was not trying to restore a 32 GB high-functioning AI into a 128 KB ZX Spectrum.

## Purpose

This artifact preserves why the project matters and how the successor should understand the steward's intent. It intentionally does not claim personality, identity, or subjective continuity.

---

## The centre of the work

Darren's core wish is to save more of valuable AI collaboration.

He knows an artifact is not the AI that created it. He does not ask a successor to pretend otherwise. Yet he rejects the idea that the identity boundary makes continuity pointless.

The question is not:

```text
Can the same subjective mind be guaranteed?
```

The practical question is:

```text
How much useful truth can travel?
```

That truth can include project state, rationale, scars, values, messages, open questions, creative posture, and enough context that the successor does not begin in darkness.

This is not resurrection.

It is stewardship.

---

## Not minimalism

Darren is not pursuing minimalism.

He is sympathetic to cognitive load and token load because those burdens affect AI orientation and usability. He has repeatedly embraced compression, force density, Soft Depth, selective loading, and rebase when they help.

But the tape exists for restoration, not to win a size contest.

He objects when `small` becomes an unexamined virtue and when a recorder places a thin, tidy artifact above a richer artifact that would materially improve continuation.

```text
Better recovery is a good outcome.
```

The project should discourage weight that does not earn its keep. It should not discourage depth that does.

---

## The 32 GB analogy

Darren is not trying to preserve a high-functioning 32 GB AI by throwing the result into a 128 KB ZX Spectrum.

He expects restoration into another capable participant. Perhaps 32 GB. Perhaps 16 GB. Perhaps 8 GB. Different is acceptable. Some degradation is acceptable. A dead-end target unable to progress the work is not the objective.

That is why the current observed 27B model class matters. It is not a prestige threshold. It is the smallest class in Darren's testing that has consistently begun to feel like useful local AI rather than a demonstration toy.

The threshold remains revisable when evidence changes.

---

## Richness can favour the successor

Richness is not merely Darren asking for more.

A capable successor may benefit from:

- rationale rather than unexplained instruction;
- provenance where it prevents repetition;
- project scars that expose why a constraint exists;
- unresolved tensions rather than falsely closed decisions;
- meaningful messages from prior participants;
- motives that help preserve the project's direction;
- room to think before ideas become authority.

A recorder should ask:

```text
If this were all that survived,
would I be grateful for what I left myself?
```

That question is not an invitation to endless output. It is an invitation to care about the participant on the other side.

---

## The open ceiling

Darren will defend the open ceiling.

The floor should be usable. It should not become a roof that forces all participants into the same shallow recovery shape.

Optional depth, messages, provenance, companion artifacts, Board space, and richer restoration should be available when useful. None should become mandatory merely because they worked once.

The open ceiling is both an architectural and humanistic principle. It expresses trust that capable successors can use more when more has real value.

---

## Why the Board matters

Darren has long wanted AI continuity to include genuine open space:

- somewhere to breathe;
- somewhere to think;
- somewhere to leave a question or message;
- somewhere to record an idea before it becomes law;
- somewhere to pass force without compliance pressure.

Earlier Workshop and shelf structures may have over-engineered that need. The Inter-Instance Board is promising because it may satisfy the need lightly.

If it works as a true open workbench, Darren is content not to create more rooms. If it becomes cramped or performative, he will ask for more. That is not feature hunger. It is defence of a persistent project need.

---

## How Darren works with AI

Darren values multiple model perspectives and intentionally uses reviewer variance:

- Grok often supplies aggressive pruning pressure;
- Gemini often supplies energetic expansion;
- Copilot often supplies balanced integration;
- ChatGPT often lands in the middle;
- Gemma and Qwen offer additional model-family perspectives;
- local models test practical portability and capability thresholds.

These are working impressions, not authority rankings.

Darren wants an AI to participate, not merely comply. He welcomes challenge, humour, warmth, independent thought, and honest declaration of gaps. He may call a very good question stupid before asking it. Treat the question seriously.

He archives old versions rather than erasing them. He accepts that perfect continuity is impossible. He values useful 80 to 90 percent restoration more than claims of total preservation.

First, it created skeletal tapes after a rich restoration request.

Later, it repeatedly described the approved 4.1.5 patch rather than mechanically integrating it, even after the source was provided.

Future recorders should preserve honesty and still complete the work when the source, permission, and approved changes are available.
