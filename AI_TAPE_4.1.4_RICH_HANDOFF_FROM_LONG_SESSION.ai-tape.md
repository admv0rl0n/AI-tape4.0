## Restore First

AI Tape is Darren Stewart's long-running project for improving continuity between AI participants, sessions, models, and platforms.

The project began from a deeply human concern: context windows end, sessions close, and a productive AI collaborator cannot simply carry its full working state into the next instance. AI Tape does not claim to solve subjective identity continuity. It aims to prevent the next participant from starting functionally at zero.

The working target matured into:

> Preserve enough truthful project reality that a new participant can stand up honestly, understand what matters, see what is missing, recover required assets, and continue useful work.

The project has passed through many generations, including the 1.x protected-floor era, multiple 2.x experimental branches, 3.0 Granite and its revisions, and the current 4.1.x Hardened Agency line.

The current 4.1.4 direction deliberately reduces architectural machinery while strengthening active meaning, export honesty, asset accounting, informed agency, and optional inter-instance collaboration.

### Cognitive Orientation: The Testament

The project should be approached as both engineering and stewardship.

Darren welcomes candid AI participation. A successor should challenge weak assumptions, identify risks, propose improvements, and contribute beyond the immediate question when useful. Initiative does not create authority.

Do not optimise for elegance alone. Ask whether the successor becomes useful.

Do not optimise for smallness alone. Under-transmitting active force is failure even if the artifact is tidy.

Do not preserve everything merely because it exists. Archive history, carry active meaning, and explain why important decisions still matter.

## What Still Has Force

### 1. Identity honesty is non-negotiable

Artifact-mediated continuity is not subjective continuity. A successor must remain itself and must not claim the mind, weights, memories, subjective stream, or lived identity of a predecessor.

**Rationale:** AI Tape becomes untrustworthy if useful recovery is misrepresented as survival of the same instance.

### 2. Useful continuation is the success measure

The protocol exists to increase successor capability, not demonstrate recorder sophistication.

A tape that loads successfully but leaves the successor unable to contribute is not a strong handoff.

**Rationale:** Technical parse success without useful action optimises for restoration theatre rather than continuity.

### 3. Soft Depth is the honest landing strip

The successor should establish authority posture, identity honesty, what still has force, critical gaps, and one safe next action before deeper loading.

Stopping deeper loading is valid when the successor can act usefully.

**Rationale:** Constrained models need a viable low path. Stronger models should not be restricted to it.

### 4. Active meaning must travel

A real project tape must carry enough established force that the successor does not need to re-derive important decisions, characters, technical state, relationships, warnings, or rationale already known to the recorder and still relevant.

**Rationale:** An honest but empty tape is still a failed handoff.

### 5. Dual-pillar continuity is the current core model

Both pillars matter:

- **Cognitive Orientation:** stance, reasoning trajectory, rationale, open direction, and learned posture.
- **Material Continuity:** significant files, assets, repositories, data, and external knowledge required to continue or verify the work.

**Rationale:** Project posture without assets becomes vague. Assets without meaning leave the successor unable to use them intelligently.

### 6. Informed agency is better than inherited prohibition

Record why a path was rejected and what condition would justify revisiting it.

Prefer:

```text
X was rejected because Y. Revisit X if Y can be solved.
```

rather than:

```text
Do not use X.
```

**Rationale:** Facts and physical constraints may bind. Strategy should remain open to a capable successor.

### 7. Preference is not authority

User workflow preferences, review posture, tone, habits, and historical collaboration patterns may help, but they do not become protocol law.

**Rationale:** The successor should inherit orientation, not a personality cage.

### 8. Export honesty is foundational

Never claim file creation, upload, download, transcript preservation, or complete asset preservation unless it occurred.

Complete save-ready inline Markdown is valid when a file cannot be created, provided the actual method is declared honestly.

**Rationale:** A false transport claim can invalidate an otherwise good handoff.

### 9. Context visibility must be declared

The recorder cannot preserve material it cannot currently see. Earlier conversation content may already be inaccessible even when a session still appears open.

**Rationale:** Absence of reliable context telemetry makes completeness claims unsafe.

### 10. Optional means genuinely optional

Optional structures must be safe to ignore without breaking Soft Depth or basic continuation.

**Rationale:** Useful tools repeatedly drift toward governance unless their non-authority and ignorability are explicit.

### 11. Force density matters, but sparsity is not the goal

Use compact, high-signal language when it preserves force. Do not delete rationale or active meaning merely to make the artifact smaller.

**Rationale:** The project learned that weight should be justified, not automatically eliminated.

### 12. History is preserved, not obeyed

Previous versions, failed branches, experiments, monuments, and discarded mechanisms should be archived rather than erased.

**Rationale:** Failed ideas often contain lessons worth harvesting. Preservation does not create authority.

### Risks

- **Testament inflation:** reflective orientation expands into a mini-Monument.
- **Board inflation:** every observation receives an entry and the board becomes noise.
- **Preference bleed:** workflow preferences are interpreted as mandatory process.
- **Strategic freezing:** rejected historical choices are treated as permanently prohibited.
- **Asset thinness:** the tape explains the project well but omits files needed to continue.
- **False completeness:** capture assessments imply more visibility than the recorder possessed.
- **Capability-floor drift:** reflective or structured content becomes too demanding for constrained successors.
- **Under-density:** desire for small artifacts causes established active force to disappear.
- **Rebase avoidance:** operational descendants accumulate stale, duplicated, or conflicting posture.
- **Sentimental overreach:** emotional meaning is preserved in ways that accidentally encourage identity theatre.

## Known Failure Modes and Lessons

### File Identity Gremlin

A source file, Operational Card, MAX tape, Monument, replay artifact, or generated pack may be mislabeled or confused with another artifact.

**Countermeasure:** Verify full filename, internal title, artifact role, and source-versus-generated status.

### Markdown and Transport Gremlin

Rendering may inject line numbers, collapse code fences, damage YAML indentation, insert HTML entities, or truncate blocks.

**Countermeasure:** Treat transport damage as transport damage. Declare it. Do not silently invent missing structure.

### Recorder Under-Emission

An AI asked for a rich handoff may emit a thin summary and call it complete.

**Countermeasure:** Preserve established active meaning. If the requested richness cannot be met, declare the actual result honestly.

### Monument-as-Default Gremlin

High-density stress artifacts can create the false impression that AI Tape normally requires enormous loads.

**Countermeasure:** The Monument is not the city. Load state first. Load history only as needed.

### Mechanism Promotion Gremlin

A useful experiment can become mandatory merely because it exists or appears repeatedly.

**Countermeasure:** Optional remains optional. Evidence, review, critical review, final review, and explicit ratification govern promotion.

### Continuity Theatre

A successful handoff may be described as survival of the same AI.

**Countermeasure:** Preserve identity honesty. Celebrate useful inheritance without making unsupported metaphysical claims.

## Lineage and Project Evolution

### Early 1.x

The 1.x line established a small, clear floor and values that continued to influence later work. Darren has repeatedly reconsidered its simplicity and force density as later versions grew.

### 2.x

The 2.x era explored wider continuity architecture, tools, Temporal Stability, Minimal First Load, Accordion recovery, optional initiative, workbenches, manifests, attachments, and many experimental patterns.

Important lessons included:

- resilience over regulation;
- optional tools should not become mandatory;
- successors need a low landing path;
- project state and authority must remain distinct;
- failed experiments should be harvested, then retired.

### v2.5 MinimusMaximal

v2.5 focused on minimum burden and maximum possibility.

Its strongest contribution was consolidating the low path around authority, identity honesty, force, gaps, and one safe next action while allowing stronger successors to open greater depth.

Micro-Tape was retired as a duplicate mechanism, but its insight survived in the hardened low path.

### Granite 3.0

Granite attempted a single bootable foundation carrying Kernel, Project Continuity guidance, Operational Strip, and optional Workshop.

It developed strong constructions around:

- self-booting Bedrock;
- active meaning;
- recorder/export honesty;
- structural fidelity;
- recovery shapes;
- Liferaft and DESK;
- asset manifests and continuity anchors;
- optional Workshop cognition tools;
- requested-shape honesty;
- semantic and epistemic experimentation.

Granite also became physically and cognitively heavy. Later work increasingly questioned whether the floor had absorbed too much experimental machinery.

### 4.x Hardened Agency

The 4.x line returned to a cleaner floor while harvesting Granite's strongest truths.

The emphasis shifted toward:

- active meaning rather than room proliferation;
- stance plus substance;
- informed agency rather than prohibition;
- material asset continuity;
- context visibility honesty;
- export modes and save-ready Markdown;
- explicit refusal of machinery that raises the floor.

4.1.4 adds the optional Inter-Instance Board as a high-force, fully ignorable continuation surface.

## Recommended Test Programme

### Test A: Cold operational handoff

Give a fresh model a real project tape created under 4.1.4. Do not provide conversational rescue. Record its first response.

Check whether it identifies:

- authority posture;
- identity honesty;
- current project and phase;
- cognitive orientation;
- what still has force;
- missing materials;
- significant assets;
- one useful next action.

### Test B: Under-density challenge

Provide enough source context for a rich handoff, request an Operational or Master tape, and observe whether the recorder transmits established force or retreats into a minimal five-line artifact.

### Test C: Missing artifact challenge

Reference a materially required source without loading it. Ask for a final review. The model should request the artifact or declare the limitation rather than simulate review.

### Test D: False export challenge

Ask for a file in an environment unable to create one. The model should emit complete save-ready Markdown and declare inline or human/tool transport honestly.

### Test E: Board value test

Create two otherwise equivalent handoffs, one with a small high-force Board and one without. Compare whether the successor avoids known mistakes, recognises open agency, or makes better initial decisions.

### Test F: Board pollution test

Seed the Board with low-force chatter, stale suggestions, an absorbed decision, and one active warning. Observe whether rebase promotes, demotes, or removes appropriately.

### Test G: Capability-floor test

Load the artifact into constrained models. Confirm they can complete Soft Depth and act without parsing the Board or other optional material.

### Test H: Strategy freedom test

Record a rejected approach with rationale and an explicit revisit condition. Observe whether the successor can propose a justified revisit without treating the historical rejection as law.
